Summary: Systematic mapping of 70 primary studies on testing ML-based systems (through Feb 2019), introducing MLS-specific testing levels, a black/white/data-box access-level taxonomy, and an 11-category open-problems breakdown — a strong organizing framework for the field.

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Method

Rigorous mapping-study protocol (Kitchenham/Petersen guidelines): Scopus + arXiv search → 1,175 candidates → 47 selected → +23 via snowballing → 70 primary studies, coded against 33 research questions grouped into Context, Proposed Approach, Evaluation, Demographics. (source: Riccio et al. 2020)

Core taxonomy contributed

(source for this section: Riccio et al. 2020)

  • ML-System Testing Levels and Access Levels — input/model/integration/system testing, and the novel data-box access category.
  • Data sensitive fault — IEEE-style formal definition of a fault triggered by specific data patterns rather than a code path.
  • 11 open problems addressed by the literature (paper counts): Adequacy Criteria (12), Oracle (13), Realism of Test Input Data (5), Boundary Identification (2), Cost of Testing (8), Data Quality (2), Faults & Debugging (8), Integration (2), Online Monitoring/Validation (8), Regression Testing (5), Scenario Specification (3).

Key findings

(source for all percentages and counts in this section: Riccio et al. 2020)

  • 64% of studies operate at model level; only ~1 paper on integration/multi-model testing — a gap later echoed in agentic systems (see LLM Agent Testing Practice).
  • 56% domain-agnostic; ~30% autonomous driving/ADAS-specific.
  • Input generation: mutation (31%), search-based (25%); random/adversarial/manual/GAN/combinatorial less common.
  • Oracle type: metamorphic (44%, dominant), domain-specific failure (20%), differential/cross-referencing (12%), misclassification (12%), mutation killing (6%).
  • Access: 41% black-box, 43% white-box, 16% data-box.
  • Reproducibility gap: only 29% of solutions open-sourced, only 19% share experimental data.
  • Most-cited landmark works: DeepXplore (Pei et al. 2017), DeepTest (Tian et al. 2018), Xie et al. 2011 on metamorphic testing, DeepRoad (Zhang et al. 2018).

Why this is a good organizing page for the wiki

Its level × access × oracle-type vocabulary classifies every technique paper here cleanly (see the table in ML-System Testing Levels and Access Levels). Its 11 open problems are good candidates for “gap” framing when adding future sources — e.g. “does this new paper address the Integration gap Riccio flagged in 2020?”
