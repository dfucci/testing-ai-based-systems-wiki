2026-09-14 — Ingest of 1 new source (Oracle Conversion)

Sources ingested:

  • Liang et al. - 2026 - Correct Tests Are Not Enough Measuring and Training Oracle Conversion in Specification-Based Test G.pdf (arXiv:2609.05879v1, 5 Sep 2026)

Pages created:

  • oracle-conversion.org — Liang et al. paper summary. Same author group/TC-Bench audit lineage as audit-feedback-driven-evolution.org. Splits a suite’s fault-detection opportunity into potential kill (input could detect a fault, if output were correct) vs. effective kill (does detect it, given the model’s own predicted output); trains an Additive-GRPO reward jointly on both plus correctness. Key result: correctness-only reward (NoKill) reaches the highest correctness/full-kill but drops input-kill below baseline — optimizing correctness alone narrows the model to less-discriminative inputs; Additive is the only arm improving correctness, input kill, effective kill, and BTS jointly (step 75 vs Base: C 28.59%→42.54%, O 24.06%→25.27%, K 12.23%→14.15%, OCE 49.70%→56.51%).

Pages updated (cross-links added):

  • oracle-problem.org — added source, added “Known pitfall: correct oracles can still fail to convert opportunity into detection” section, added related-pages link
  • audit-feedback-driven-evolution.org — added forward cross-link to the follow-up paper, bumped date
  • test-adequacy-criteria.org — added source, added “Potential vs. effective kill: decomposing mutation-style scores” section, added related-pages link
  • index.org — added oracle-conversion.org row under “Oracle construction and auditing”

Not changed: no update to metamorphic-testing.org or agent-evaluation.org — this paper’s RL-training/reward-decomposition angle is closer to test-adequacy-criteria.org’s kill-score discussion than to those pages’ existing content.
